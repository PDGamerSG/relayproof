PROBLEM STATEMENT 05
Build a Pre-Meeting Briefing Agent
Preparing for important meetings often requires researching companies, individuals, recent announcements,
leadership changes, and other developments. This information is scattered across multiple sources, making
manual research time-consuming, and AI-generated summaries can miss important updates or present
unsupported information as fact.
The challenge is to build an agent that gathers recent, relevant information and turns it into a concise,
source-backed briefing.
GOAL
Build an AI-powered research agent that takes a company or person and a meeting time as input,
then produces a one-page briefing covering relevant developments from the previous 30 days.
CORE REQUIREMENTS
/ Meeting context: Accept a company or person and the
scheduled meeting time.
/ Recent research: Identify relevant developments from the
preceding 30 days, including news, launches, hires, and other
meaningful updates.
/ Concise briefing: Produce a structured, one-page report that
helps the user prepare for the meeting.
/ Source attribution: Include sources for factual claims so the
user can verify the information.
/ Open questions: Highlight relevant questions or discussion
points that may be useful during the meeting.
EXPECTED OUTCOME
A working meeting-preparation agent that transforms recent web research into a concise, reliable, source-backed
briefing that helps users enter conversations better informed.
OPTIONAL IMPLEMENTATION GUIDANCE
Participants may explore briefings for sales calls, partnership discussions, investor meetings, or business development conversations.
Tavily can provide web search and research capabilities, while Bifrost can manage model requests and related gateway functionality. The
agent should distinguish verified findings from uncertain information and avoid inventing unsupported details.
